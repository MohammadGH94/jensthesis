# Chapter 2 — Age-Specific Reference Intervals

**Detailed map:** [[Chapter 02 - Map]]

Discrete reference intervals for plasma biomarkers in a Canadian population, ages 3–79.

## Cohort & methods

- Cohort: [[CHMS]] (Canadian Health Measures Survey)
- Platform: [[Quanterix Simoa]] — N4PE and p-tau-181 V2 advantage assays
- Analyte set: [[Aβ42-40]], [[p-tau-181]], [[NfL]], [[GFAP]]
- Statistics: discrete reference interval bins by age decade
- Cross-lot comparison performed (precursor to [[Chapter 04 - Cross Lot Analysis]])

## Headline finding

All four biomarkers show **U-shaped** trajectories across the lifespan, with elevated concentrations in early childhood, a low plateau through middle adulthood, and a steep rise in older adults.
