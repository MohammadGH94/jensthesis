# Chapter 8 — Co-pathologies in an Autopsy Cohort

Exploratory work on detecting α-synuclein, [[TDP-43 Pathology]], and cerebrovascular disease alongside [[ADNC]].

## Cohort & methods

- Cohort: [[CARD]] (autopsy)
- Platform: [[Alamar ARGO]] / [[NULISA]] multiplex panel
- Statistics: [[LASSO and Elastic Net]] feature selection
- Two analyses:
  1. Detection of each co-pathology (α-syn, TDP-43, CVD) in the full cohort (n = 84)
  2. Detection of co-pathologies among cases that already have ADNC (n = 56)

## Headline findings

- Distinct biomarker signatures retained for each co-pathology
- Some biomarkers appear common across analyses, suggesting shared neurodegenerative axes
- Exploratory — sets directions for follow-on work
