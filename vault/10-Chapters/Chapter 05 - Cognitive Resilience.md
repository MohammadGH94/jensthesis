# Chapter 5 — Cognitive Resilience

Phenotyping cognitively resilient elderly using plasma biomarkers.

## Cohort & methods

- Cohort: [[Super Seniors]] (provided by [[Angela Brooks-Wilson]] and Sherry Leach)
- Compared to [[CHMS]] reference curves from [[Chapter 03 - Reference Curves]]
- Platform: [[Quanterix Simoa]]

## Headline finding

Super Seniors show "**resilient**" levels of [[Aβ42-40]] and [[p-tau-181]] (i.e. AD-pathology-protective profiles), but [[NfL]] and [[GFAP]] tracking the normative population — suggesting they're protected from AD pathology specifically rather than generic neurodegeneration.
