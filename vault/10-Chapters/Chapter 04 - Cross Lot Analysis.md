# Chapter 4 — Cross-Lot Analysis of Simoa Assays

A methods/QC chapter that underpins every other data chapter using [[Quanterix Simoa]].

## What it does

- Quality control across N4PE and p-tau-181 V2 advantage assay lots
- Cross-lot comparisons for [[Aβ42-40]], [[p-tau-181]], [[NfL]], [[GFAP]]
- Reference sample analysis to anchor results across years and lots

## Why it matters

Plasma biomarker results must be comparable across lots and over time for clinical use. This chapter demonstrates the robustness needed for [[Clinical Implementation in Canada]].
