# Chapter 9 — COMPASS-ND Prevalence

**Detailed map:** [[Chapter 09 - Map]]

The integration chapter — combines [[Chapter 03 - Reference Curves|reference curves]] and [[Chapter 07 - Autopsy AD Neuropathology|autopsy cut-offs]] into [[Plasma Probability Scores]] applied to [[COMPASS-ND]].

## Cohort & methods

- Cohort: [[COMPASS-ND]] participants and CSF sub-cohort
- Platform: [[Quanterix Simoa]]
- Two PPS approaches:
  - [[Plasma Probability Scores|RC-PPS]] — derived from CHMS reference curves (pre-dementia indication)
  - [[Plasma Probability Scores|ADNC-PPS]] — derived from CARD autopsy cut-offs (dementia indication)

## Headline findings

- Plasma probability scores can estimate AD pathology prevalence at population scale
- Useful in pre-dementia and dementia stages, with separate score variants for each
- Concordance and discordance between clinical diagnosis and PPS profiled
