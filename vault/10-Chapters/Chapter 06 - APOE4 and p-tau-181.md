# Chapter 6 — APOE4 Modifies p-tau-181 in Super-Seniors

A focused study of [[APOE4]] genotype effects on [[p-tau-181]] in cognitively healthy [[Super Seniors]].

## Cohort & methods

- Cohort: [[Super Seniors]] dichotomised by APOE4 carrier status
- Platform: [[Quanterix Simoa]]
- Variables: plasma biomarkers, demographics, APOE genotype

## Headline finding

APOE ε4 carrier status modifies [[p-tau-181]] concentrations even in cognitively healthy super-seniors, with implications for biomarker interpretation in APOE4 carriers.
