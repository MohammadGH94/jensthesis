# NULISA

Multiplex protein quantification chemistry that runs on the [[Alamar ARGO]] platform — used in [[Chapter 07 - Autopsy AD Neuropathology]] and [[Chapter 08 - Co-pathologies]].

Distinct from [[Quanterix Simoa]]: NULISA gives breadth (many proteins, modest depth per protein), Simoa gives depth (very high sensitivity for the four canonical analytes).
