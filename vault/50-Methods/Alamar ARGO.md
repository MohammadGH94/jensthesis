# Alamar ARGO

Multiplex platform running the [[NULISA]] (Nucleic acid Linked Immuno-Sandwich Assay) panel.

## Used in

- [[Chapter 07 - Autopsy AD Neuropathology]] — staging beyond the four canonical biomarkers
- [[Chapter 08 - Co-pathologies]] — broad protein panel for [[LASSO and Elastic Net]] feature selection

## Why it matters

A NULISA panel measures hundreds of CNS-relevant proteins simultaneously, enabling **discovery** of biomarker signatures for [[Co-pathologies]] — α-syn, [[TDP-43 Pathology|TDP-43]], CVD — that single-analyte assays can't capture.
