# Clinical Implementation in Canada

The integrating thread of the entire thesis — Canada-specific translation of plasma biomarkers into routine clinical practice.

## What needs to be in place

1. **Standardised assays** with known reproducibility ([[Chapter 04 - Cross Lot Analysis]])
2. **Reference norms** for interpretation ([[Chapter 02 - Reference Intervals]], [[Chapter 03 - Reference Curves]])
3. **Pathological validation** ([[Chapter 07 - Autopsy AD Neuropathology]])
4. **Clinical scores** that convert raw measurements to actionable probabilities ([[Plasma Probability Scores]], [[Chapter 09 - COMPASS-ND Prevalence]])
5. **Regulatory pathway** — IVDs, LDTs, [[CADTH]], MDD
6. **Health-economic justification** — QALYs, BIA, ICER ([[Chapter 10 - Discussion]])

## The Canadian-specific angle

- Public, single-payer system
- Distinct regulatory structure (Health Canada / MDD)
- Provincial laboratory medicine services (PLMS) decide adoption
- HTA via [[CADTH]]

See [[Chapter 10 - Discussion]] §10.3 and §10.4.
