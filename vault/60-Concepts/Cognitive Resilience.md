# Cognitive Resilience

The phenomenon of remaining cognitively intact despite advanced age and / or pathological burden.

## Operationalised in this thesis as

- The [[Super Seniors]] phenotype
- A pattern in plasma biomarkers: **resilient** [[Aβ42-40]] / [[p-tau-181]] paired with **normal-aging** [[NfL]] / [[GFAP]] (see [[Chapter 05 - Cognitive Resilience]])
- Modulated by [[APOE4]] genotype (see [[Chapter 06 - APOE4 and p-tau-181]])
