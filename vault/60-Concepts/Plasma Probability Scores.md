# Plasma Probability Scores (PPS)

A probabilistic score combining plasma biomarker results into a single AD-pathology probability — the deliverable of [[Chapter 09 - COMPASS-ND Prevalence]].

## Two flavours

### RC-PPS — Reference-Curve PPS
- Built from [[CHMS]] reference curves ([[Chapter 03 - Reference Curves]])
- Best for **pre-dementia** indication (deviation from age-norms)

### ADNC-PPS — ADNC-anchored PPS
- Built from [[CARD]] autopsy cut-offs ([[Chapter 07 - Autopsy AD Neuropathology]])
- Best for **dementia-stage** indication (probability of AD pathology)

## Applied in

- [[Chapter 09 - COMPASS-ND Prevalence]] — both variants estimate AD prevalence in [[COMPASS-ND]]

## Why it matters

PPS is the bridge between research-grade plasma biomarker measurements and a clinically usable, interpretable result — a key step in [[Clinical Implementation in Canada]].
