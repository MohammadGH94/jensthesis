# Frontotemporal Dementia (FTD)

Non-AD dementia with FTLD pathology. Subtypes include behavioural variant FTD (bvFTD), primary progressive aphasia (PPA), and others.

- Discussed in [[Chapter 01 - Introduction]] as a non-AD differential
- Plasma [[NfL]] is particularly informative for FTD
