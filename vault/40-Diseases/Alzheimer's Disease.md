# Alzheimer's Disease

The thesis's primary focus. AD is the leading cause of dementia and the disease most amenable to plasma biomarker diagnosis.

## Pathological hallmarks

- Amyloid plaques → measured by [[Aβ42-40]]
- Tau tangles → measured by [[p-tau-181]]
- Neurodegeneration → measured by [[NfL]]
- Neuroinflammation → measured by [[GFAP]]

Together: [[ADNC]] (Alzheimer's Disease Neuropathologic Change).

## Diagnostic frameworks

- [[ATN Framework]] — biological definition
- Clinical: cognitive testing (MMSE, MoCA, CDR), neuroimaging
- Fluid: CSF and plasma biomarkers

## Variants

- Late-onset AD (LOAD)
- Early-onset AD (EOAD)
- Frontal variant of AD (fvAD)
- Posterior cortical atrophy (PCA)
- Logopenic progressive aphasia (lvPPA)

## Risk modifiers

- [[APOE4]] — strongest genetic risk factor
- Age — strongest overall risk factor
