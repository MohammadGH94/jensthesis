# Co-pathologies

Most autopsy cases of dementia show **multiple** neuropathologies, not pure AD. The mixed-pathology problem motivates [[Chapter 08 - Co-pathologies]].

## Key co-pathologies

- α-synuclein ([[Dementia with Lewy Bodies]])
- [[TDP-43 Pathology]] (LATE, FTLD-TDP)
- Cerebrovascular disease ([[Vascular Dementia]], [[Cerebral Amyloid Angiopathy]])
- Hippocampal sclerosis (HS)
- Argyrophilic grain disease (AGD)
