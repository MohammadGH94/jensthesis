# Neuroinflammation

Inflammatory and reactive-glial response in the CNS, indexed in plasma by [[GFAP]] (astrocytic) and explored in the [[NULISA]] panel. Discussed as a fourth pathological axis (alongside amyloid, tau, neurodegeneration) in [[Chapter 01 - Introduction]].
