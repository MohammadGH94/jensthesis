# TDP-43 Pathology

TAR DNA-binding protein 43 inclusions — central to FTLD-TDP and LATE (Limbic-predominant Age-related TDP-43 Encephalopathy).

## Relevance

- Co-pathology in many AD cases — addressed in [[Chapter 08 - Co-pathologies]]
- Targeted via novel biomarkers in the [[NULISA]] panel
- Distinct from [[Frontotemporal Dementia|tau-positive FTD]] subtypes
