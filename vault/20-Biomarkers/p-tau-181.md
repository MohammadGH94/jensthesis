# p-tau-181

Phosphorylated tau at threonine 181 — the most clinically established plasma tau biomarker for [[Alzheimer's Disease]].

## Why it matters

Plasma p-tau-181 rises with brain tau pathology and is specific to AD-type tauopathy (the "T" in the [[ATN Framework]]).
