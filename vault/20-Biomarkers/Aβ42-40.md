# Aβ42/40

The plasma amyloid-β 42/40 ratio — a low ratio reflects brain amyloidosis (a hallmark of [[Alzheimer's Disease]]).

## Why a ratio?

Aβ42 declines in plasma when it deposits in the brain; Aβ40 is more stable. The 42/40 **ratio** controls for total Aβ production and improves AD specificity.
