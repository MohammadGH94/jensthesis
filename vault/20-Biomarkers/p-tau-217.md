# p-tau-217

Phosphorylated tau at threonine 217 — a newer plasma tau biomarker that has emerged as one of the strongest single discriminators for Alzheimer's pathology.

## Where it appears in the thesis

- [[Chapter 07 - Autopsy AD Neuropathology]] — measured on the [[NULISA]] panel; **best single discriminator** of significant ADNC (AUROC 0.84). Combined with [[Aβ42-40]] reaches AUROC 0.88; with [[Aβ42-40]] + [[p-tau-181]] + [[GFAP]] reaches AUROC 0.91.
- Mentioned in [[Chapter 01 - Introduction]] as one of the clinically best-performing AD plasma biomarkers alongside [[Aβ42-40]].

## How it differs from [[p-tau-181]]

Both are AD-specific tau biomarkers measured in plasma. p-tau-217 generally shows **larger fold-changes** with brain amyloid burden and is the more clinically established test in the most recent landscape. p-tau-181 is the workhorse on the [[Quanterix Simoa]] platform across this thesis; p-tau-217 enters via the [[Alamar ARGO]] / [[NULISA]] panel in [[Chapter 07 - Autopsy AD Neuropathology|Chapter 7]].
