# COMPASS-ND

The Canadian multi-centre prospective dementia cohort — the real-world deployment target.

## Used in

- [[Chapter 09 - COMPASS-ND Prevalence]] — applies [[Plasma Probability Scores]] to estimate AD prevalence in pre-dementia and dementia stages

## Why it matters

COMPASS-ND is part of the [[CCNA]] (Canadian Consortium on Neurodegeneration in Aging). Demonstrating that plasma biomarkers + PPS work in this cohort is a major step toward [[Clinical Implementation in Canada]].
