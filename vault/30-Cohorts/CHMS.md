# CHMS — Canadian Health Measures Survey

The normative population cohort — the foundation for everything age-referenced in this thesis.

## Profile

- Statistics Canada's flagship national health survey
- Plasma samples spanning ages **3–79**
- Analysed at the UBC Statistics Canada Research Data Center
- Forms the **denominator** for what "normal" looks like
