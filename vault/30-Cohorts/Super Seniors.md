# Super Seniors

Cognitively healthy elderly — the resilience cohort. Original study by [[Angela Brooks-Wilson]] and Sherry Leach (BC Cancer / SFU).

## Used in

- [[Chapter 05 - Cognitive Resilience]] — biomarker phenotyping vs. [[CHMS]] norms
- [[Chapter 06 - APOE4 and p-tau-181]] — APOE4 stratified analysis (published)

## Why important

Super-seniors who reach advanced age cognitively intact may carry biological signatures of resilience to AD. This thesis uses plasma biomarkers to ask: are they protected from AD pathology specifically, or from neurodegeneration broadly?

**Answer:** AD-specific protection — see [[Chapter 05 - Cognitive Resilience]].
